The Room – dinner, kept close
Quiet. Settled. Yours.

Paris once kept its most private dinners behind a cabinet door.

At FC, The Room holds a long white-clothed table for up to eight. The menu is settled with the kitchen in advance and served there, to no one but your table. When the evening belongs somewhere else, Bespoke Catering brings the FC kitchen to your own venue.

A business dinner that should stay between partners. An anniversary meant only for family.

Some evenings are best kept close, wherever close happens to be.
