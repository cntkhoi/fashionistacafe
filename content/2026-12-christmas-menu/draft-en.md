Christmas Menu – nine courses, and no hurry to leave the table
Long. Slow. Shared.

The French réveillon takes its name from veiller, to stay awake, for a feast that runs deep into the night. Through December, in a French villa in Saigon, that pace returns across nine courses. Yellowtail amberjack arrives cool, beside basil sorbet. Pho, eel and consommé meet where France and Vietnam share a table. Sendai wagyu takes Café de Paris butter. Ben Tre chocolate, then petit fours, close the night.

Perhaps the best nights are the ones no one wants to end.
